We're not lawyers - this is a plain explanation of what YardWatch is and what to expect from using it. By using the site, you're agreeing to this.

What YardWatch Is

YardWatch checks the public inventory listings at Jalopy Jungle (all 5 Idaho locations) and Trusty Pick-A-Part several times a day, and lets you search that inventory, save a watchlist of makes/models/years you're after, and get emailed when something matching shows up.

Not Affiliated With the Yards

YardWatch is an independent, unofficial tool. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Jalopy Jungle or Trusty Pick-A-Part. We just read their public inventory pages.

Inventory Data Might Be Wrong

Everything you see on YardWatch - what's in stock, what row it's in, whether it's still there - comes from scraping the yards' own websites on a schedule. It can be out of date, incomplete, or just wrong: a car could sell, get moved, or already be picked apart between our last check and when you show up. Always treat YardWatch as a heads-up, not a guarantee, and confirm with the yard before making a trip.

Your Account
